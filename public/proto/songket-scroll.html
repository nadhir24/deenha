Songket

Threads of ceremony, translated for today. A heritage textile woven with metallic threads, now reimagined as a scarf you can wear every day.

The Motif

Floral geometry, muted gold, and slate blue.

Each Songket scarf carries the rhythm of hand-woven flowers and vine work. We kept the palette restrained so the heritage stays wearable — not costume.

The Drape

Soft enough to fold, structured enough to hold.

The weave gives it a subtle weight. It sits well on the shoulder, falls clean in a headscarf, and softens after the first few wears.
